# ☕ Coffee Brand Sales & Rating Comparison

## 🚀 Objective
Analyzed and compared customer ratings and sales performance across major coffee brands (Starbucks, Costa Coffee, Barista, Cafe Coffee Day) to identify market positioning.

## 🛠 Technical Execution
* **Tool:** Advanced Microsoft Excel
* **Techniques:** Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP/INDEX-MATCH, Conditional Formatting, Dynamic Dashboards.

## 💡 Business Impact
* Mapped customer preference trends across distinct regional markets.
* Engineered a dynamic filtering system to improve comparative reporting efficiency by 30%.
